### QR Code Types (15 Supported)

- **VCARD** - Digital business cards
- **EMAIL** - Pre-filled email composer
- **SMS** - Pre-filled text messages
- **WIFI** - WiFi network credentials
- **TEXT** - Plain text display
- **PHONE** - Click-to-call numbers
- **LOCATION** - Geographic coordinates
- **EVENT** - Calendar events
- **LANDING_PAGE** - Custom landing pages
- **MENU** - Restaurant/business menus
- **PRODUCT** - Product information
- **PAYMENT_LINK** - Payment QR codes
- **FILE_DOWNLOAD** - Downloadable files

- **Download Formats** - PNG, SVG, PDF export
- **Logo Upload** - Add custom logos to QR codes
- **Status Management** - Active, inactive, banned status
