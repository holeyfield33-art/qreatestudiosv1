# Landing Pages Quick Start Guide

## 🚀 Getting Started in 3 Steps

### Step 1: Navigate to Landing Pages
1. Open dashboard: `/dashboard`
2. Click "Landing Pages" in sidebar (Globe icon)
3. Click "Create Landing Page" button

### Step 2: Create Your Page
**Name & URL** (30 seconds)
- Enter page name: "My Business"
- Auto-generated slug: `my-business`
- Your URL: `https://qreatestudios.com/p/my-business`
- Click "Next: Choose Template"

**Choose Template** (30 seconds)
- Browse 6 beautiful templates
- Click one to select
- Click "Next: Review"

**Review & Create** (10 seconds)
- Confirm details
- Click "Create Landing Page"
- Done! Redirects to editor

### Step 3: Publish & Share
1. Edit content in editor (or use template as-is)
2. Toggle "Published" to make live
3. Share public URL or link to QR code
4. Watch views count up!

## 🔗 Linking to QR Codes

### Method 1: From QR Code
1. Go to: `/dashboard/qr-codes/[id]`
2. Find "Connect to Landing Page" section
3. Select landing page from dropdown
4. QR now redirects to your landing page

### Method 2: From Landing Page
1. Copy landing page URL: `/p/[slug]`
2. Create new QR code
3. Choose "URL" type
4. Paste landing page URL
5. Generate QR code

## 🆘 Common Issues

### "Landing page limit reached"
**Problem**: Can't create more pages  
**Solution**: Upgrade plan or delete unused pages

### "This URL is already taken"
**Problem**: Slug not unique  
**Solution**: Choose different slug

### "Page not found" on public URL
**Problem**: Page not published  
**Solution**: Toggle "Published" in editor

### View count not updating
**Problem**: Caching or delay  
**Solution**: Wait a few seconds and refresh
